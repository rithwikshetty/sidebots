It runs on your computer.

Sidebots sits quietly on your computer and runs your agents on the agent harness you already use, Claude Code or Codex, with the skills and connections you have there. They use your own subscription or API key.

  • No accounts, analytics or ads.
  • Chats, memory and files stay on your computer.
  • Notifications are encrypted so only your phone can read them.

Jobs that run without you.

Ask an agent to remind you of something, brief you every morning, or check a price every hour. It keeps the schedule, and only texts you when it has something to say.

Set up in three steps.

  1. Open Sidebots on your computer. It starts when your computer does, and updates itself.
  2. Choose how your agents sign in. Use the agent harness already signed in on your computer, Claude Code or Codex, or an API key.
  3. Scan the code with your phone. After that your phone reaches your computer from anywhere, on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Know this first.

Agents act on their own.
They run as you on your computer, so they can read and change your files and run commands. The harness's own safety check decides what is safe to run. What it can't decide, the agent asks you on your phone.
Your computer has to be awake.
While it is asleep or off, agents wait. Your messages reach them when it wakes.
You pay for the model.
Sidebots is free and open source. Your agents use your own subscription or API key.
Your messages go to the model.
To answer you, an agent sends them to the company behind its harness, Anthropic for Claude Code or OpenAI for Codex, under your own login or key. Read the privacy policy.
