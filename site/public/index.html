A messaging app for your agents.

Each chat is an agent. Text it a job and get on with your day. It does the work on your computer and texts you back when it's done.

Download for Mac Read the source

For macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon. The phone app is coming to the App Store.

How it works.

One reply, when the work is done.

Send a job as text, photos or files. The agent works on your computer for as long as it takes, then replies once, with a notification. You don't watch it think.

It shows you what is better seen than read.

With its reply, an agent can send a page it made, a picture or a file. It opens beside the chat, so you can look and keep talking.

It runs on your computer.

Sidebots sits quietly on your computer and runs your agents on the agent harness you already use, Claude Code or Codex, with the skills and connections you have there. They use your own subscription or API key.

  • No accounts or ads.
  • Chats, memory and files stay on your computer.
  • Connect Gmail, Google Calendar and Notion, and your agents can use them as you.
  • Notifications are encrypted so only your phone can read them.

Jobs that run without you.

Ask an agent to remind you of something, brief you every morning, or check a price every hour. It keeps the schedule, and only texts you when it has something to say.

And from your phone.

Pair your phone by scanning a code in the window's Settings. Then text your agents from anywhere, and see one at work on your Lock Screen until its reply arrives.

Set up in three steps.

  1. Open Sidebots on your computer. It starts when your computer does, and updates itself.
  2. Choose how your agents sign in. Use the agent harness already signed in on your computer, Claude Code or Codex, or an API key.
  3. Text your agents in its window. To text them from your phone too, scan the code it shows. Your phone then reaches your computer from anywhere, on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Know this first.

Agents act on their own.
They run as you on your computer, so they can read and change your files and run commands. The harness's own safety check decides what is safe to run. What it can't decide, the agent asks you in its chat.
Your computer has to be awake.
While it is asleep or off, agents wait. Your messages reach them when it wakes.
You pay for the model.
Sidebots is free and open source. Your agents use your own subscription or API key.
Your messages go to the model.
To answer you, an agent sends them to the company behind its harness, Anthropic for Claude Code or OpenAI for Codex, under your own login or key. Read the privacy policy.
